Voice command test

Checks whether your voice can be detected while the app is playing speech. Takes about 1.5 minutes. Run it once on the phone speaker and once with earbuds. Keep the phone volume where you normally listen.

Audio output now:

Order: echo cancellation ON, then OFF. Each has three steps: (A) speech plays, you stay silent; (B) speech plays, you say "pause, next, resume"; (C) no playback, you say the same.
